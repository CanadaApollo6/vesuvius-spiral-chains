Reading the picture
  • Bright bands = papyrus sheets. Dark = air gaps.
  • A and B each sit on a sheet. Do not count those two.
  • Count sheets that cross the corridor between A and B.
  • Follow each sheet from far away into the corridor: two sheets that merge into one thick band still count as two.
  • The side view shows the same sheets from another angle; they often separate there.
  • A ring on a double band (two sheets touching)? Take the ring as sitting on the left sheet of the pair. So the right sheet of A's pair counts as between; the right sheet of B's pair does not.
