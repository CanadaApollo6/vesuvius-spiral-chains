# 2026-10-18: PHerc0139 9.362 um merge-sensitive test set (per-winding instance labels)

Plan (a) of `notes/2026-10-18-w1-data-recon.md`. This is a real ESRF 9.362 um test set with one uint16 instance per
segment mesh (winding). It is built to test whether a model keeps adjacent wraps apart where they touch.
- Data: `data/eval/p0139_9um/`, kept local and private (licence, section 8).
- Scripts:
  - `scripts/p0139_testset.py` (mine / select / build / finalize);
  - `scripts/p0139_preview.py` (PNGs);
  - `eval/p0139_sites.py` (loader and scoring pairs);
  - `eval/winding_sites.py --dataset p0139` (scorer).
- No GPU was used. Downloads are streamed crops only:
  - about 17.5 GB of zarr chunks, an upper bound computed as chunks touched × 2.1 MB;
  - 0.95 GB of tifxyz meshes.
  - Disk use: 2.6 GB for the set and its working files, plus 0.95 GB of meshes.

## 0. Headline
1. **87 sites of 192³ voxels**, all passing the quality gates:
   - 29 compressed;
   - 35 mixed (contact zones in otherwise open crops);
   - 23 wide controls.
   - 64 of 87 sites contain sheet contact.
   - 31 segments (windings w023-w059) appear. Sites have a median of 11 instances and a per-crop median adjacent spacing of 14.4 vox (p10-p90 11.5-18.1).
2. **Gates.**
   - 200 mined candidates gave 87 passes (63 of 140 mined-compressed, 24 of 60 mined-wide).
   - NCC is not the bottleneck: in-set NCC is 0.91-0.97, median 0.963.
   - The binding gates are labels off papyrus (97 fails) and meshes crossing dark gaps or each other (90 fails).
   - Under strict papyrus rules (every instance ≥ 0.8 within 1 vox of material) only 3 of 200 would pass.
3. **Scoring is not gameable by a constant pitch.**
   - Pairs have expected steps 0, 1 and 2-4, and are distance-balanced.
   - The metric is absolute: |delta - expected| < 0.25.
   - A GT plumbing check, a best constant pitch and a per-crop oracle pitch are scored by the same code.
   - A model must beat the oracle pitch (section 6).
4. **Two caveats.**
   - The meshes are loose: about 10% of mesh voxels lie more than 2 vox off papyrus and are moved to ignore.
   - Many papyrus layers in a crop carry no label (median 8% of material, p90 66%). The set is suited to pairwise winding tests and instance scoring of the labelled sheets only. It cannot score unlabelled false positives (section 9).

## 1. Sources and registration
- **9.362 um volume:** `s3://vesuvius-challenge-open-data/PHerc0139/volumes/20250728140407-9.362um-1.2m-113keV-masked.zarr`, level 0 (20974×6621×6621 uint8, 128³ chunks). This is the CT in `imagesTr`; it is never resampled.
- **Meshes:** `PHerc0139/segments/<segment>/mesh/<id>-on-20260102150214-2.399um.tifxyz` (48 um grid step).
  - The 9.362 meshes are not used: their 187 um step is too coarse for a 1-vox raster.
  - They are mapped with the published affine `transform_0139_2p399_to_9p362.json`, where p9_xyz = A·p2399_xyz + t.
- **Per-site shift.** The published affine is locally off by 0.8-2.3 vox (p10-p90; median 1.4 vox, 13 um).
  - Fix: a translation search maximises NCC between the 9.362 crop and the 2.399 L2 crop (4³ block mean, verified r = 0.99997 against L0) after affine mapping and blurring.
  - Search steps: ±6 vox at step 2, then ±2 at step 1, then parabolic sub-voxel refinement.
  - The shift is added to the mesh coordinates.
  - NCC with the published affine alone has a median of 0.84; after the shift it is 0.963.
  - Eight octant re-fits check that one translation is enough: in-set max deviation has a median of 0.38 vox and a maximum of 1.5.
  - The previous agent's w047 control shift (-2.26, 1.33, 1.53) is reproduced exactly.
- **Provenance in `sites.json`, per site:**
  - origin_zyx, shift, NCC (refined, published, valid fraction), octant shifts;
  - the instance-id map: id = global segment id = 1-based index of the sorted segment names, plus winding and segment name;
  - all gate measurements, the cleaning counts, the adjacent pairs with spacing statistics, coverage, and fail reasons.
  - Global keys: volumes, transform (A, t, url), segment list with blacklist reasons, gate constants, stats.
  - Dropped sites are listed in `not_in_set` with their reasons.

## 2. Site selection
- **Mining** (`mine`):
  - Takes every 4th mesh vertex of all 38 segments that have meshes. The second w030 has none. The title segment never co-occurs with other segments.
  - For each vertex: perpendicular distance to the nearest vertex of every other segment, measured point-to-plane along that segment's normal, with lateral offset < 4 vox and distance ≤ 25 vox.
  - Recorded as d_adj (adjacent winding) and d_any.
- **Spacing classes** (recto-to-recto, 9.362 vox):

  | class | spacing | in um |
  |---|---|---|
  | conflict | d < 1 | |
  | compressed | 1 ≤ d < 4 | < 37 um |
  | intermediate | 4 ≤ d < 8 | |
  | wide | 8 ≤ d < 24 | |

  d < 1 is physically impossible (thinner than one sheet), so it means coincident meshes.
- **Blacklist.** Segments that coincide with another (< 1 vox) on > 20% of points: w045/w046 (82%) and w041/w042 (33%).
  - The first naive ranking of "compressed" sites was exactly these label errors. All four segments are excluded.
- **Base requirements for a crop:**
  - inside the volume;
  - ≥ 4 windings with ≥ 40 points each in the core, and no gaps in the winding sequence;
  - ≥ 300 adjacent-pair points;
  - no blacklisted segment and no coincident (d < 1) point;
  - no range-edge winding (w023, w059), whose outer neighbour is unlabelled.
- **Choosing candidates:**
  - Compressed: ≥ 30 points with 1 ≤ d_adj < 4 and ≥ 4% of adjacent points, taken greedily by compressed fraction.
  - Wide: < 0.5% compressed and ≥ 90% at ≥ 8 vox, in seeded random order.
  - Cores are disjoint (centres ≥ 144 vox apart).
  - Result: 140 compressed and 60 wide candidates, plus 3 known controls.
- **Final site class**, set after building from the cleaned rasters' ray samples (the same rays as the scoring legacy block):

  | class | rule |
  |---|---|
  | compressed | ≥ 100 samples at 1-4 vox and ≥ 3% of samples |
  | mixed | ≥ 40 samples and ≥ 0.5% |
  | wide | < 0.5% compressed and ≥ 80% at ≥ 8 vox |

  - The raster's compressed fraction is about 1/3 of the mining proxy, so most mined-compressed sites land in "mixed".
  - "Mixed" was added after the first finalize gave only 29 compressed + 23 wide = 52 sites. All 35 sites that failed only on class qualified as mixed.
  - The site class is only a stratum. Scoring stratifies every pair by its own local spacing (contact vs open).
- **Crop size 192³ (1.8 mm), core margin 24 (core 144³):**
  - It tiles exactly into 128³ windows at stride 64 (2×2×2), the model's inference geometry.
  - Any scoring pair up to 48 vox apart fits in one tile.
  - Over 192³ a single translation still fits (octant deviation ≤ 1.5 vox). 256³ would be 2.4× the download, with more mesh drift and label holes per crop.
  - 192³ also leaves room for 60+ disjoint sites in the compressed zones.

## 3. Quality gates
Order of operations: register, rasterize the raw meshes, run the gates on the raw raster, clean (surface voxels > 2 vox from material move to ignore), then compute spacing on the cleaned raster.

Material is Otsu on the CT after Gaussian σ 0.7; distance is EDT to material.

| gate | rule (drop if) | sites failing (of 200; a site can fail several) |
|---|---|---|
| ncc | NCC < 0.90 or octant deviation > 2 vox | 4 (only gate: 1) |
| papyrus | pooled share of label voxels ≤ 1 vox from material < 0.70; any instance < 0.50 on papyrus or > 30% removed as > 2 vox off | 97 (only gate: 20) |
| crossing | mesh normal within 45° of the CT structure-tensor normal on < 70% of an instance; > 2 tiles of 24³ in which > 50% of an instance is > 4 vox from material (mesh cutting through a dark gap); paired points on the minority side of the neighbour > 5% (crossing) or < 1 vox apart > 5% (coincident); winding order flip along the normal | 90 (only gate: 15) |
| pairs | no adjacent pair with ≥ 30 samples | 0 |
| class | neither compressed, mixed nor wide | 0 (35 before "mixed" existed) |

- **Pass rates:**
  - overall 87 of 200 (43.5%);
  - mined-compressed 63 of 140;
  - mined-wide 24 of 60.
  - Strict-papyrus pass (all instances ≥ 0.8 on papyrus, no 24³ tile > 50% off by 2 vox): 3 of 200. This is reported per site as `strict_papyrus_pass`.
- **Why the tiered policy instead of the strict rule.** Diagnostics on 111 sites ruled out a registration fault:
  - the shift sign was verified by CT profile;
  - per-octant shifts give on-papyrus 0.588 vs 0.587;
  - the Otsu threshold barely matters.
  - So the meshes really are loose by 1-4 vox in places. Loose stretches are cleaned to ignore; sites where a mesh runs through a gap are dropped.
- **In-set quality:**
  - pooled on-papyrus 0.77 / 0.85 / 0.90 (p10/p50/p90);
  - share of raw label voxels removed as off-papyrus: median 10% over all built sites.
- **Controls:**
  - The w023 site passes (NCC 0.951). It is not in the set, because w023 is a range edge.
  - The w037 site fails papyrus: pooled 0.54, w41 at 0.24. Its meshes run through a wide dark gap; preview below.
  - The w047 site fails crossing: it contains w041/w042 and w045/w046, which are coincident or crossing, plus order flips.

## 5. Label convention
**jeong200** (measured with `scripts/w1_label_stats.band_stats`; `notes/2026-10-18-w1-data-recon.md`):
- binary {0,1};
- a 3-vox band (EDT thickness 3.0-3.5);
- centred 0.2-1.2 vox inside the recto half-max edge.

**Ours:**

| file | content |
|---|---|
| `surfacesTr/<stem>.tif` (uint16) | 1-vox raster of each mesh. Bilinear quad subdivision ×12, rint to voxels; the closest sample wins a contested voxel. id = segment id, 0 = none. Voxels > 2 vox from material are removed. |
| `instancesTr/<stem>.tif` (uint16) | surfacesTr grown to a band: voxels within EDT 1.0 of a surface voxel, carrying the id of the nearest surface. |
| `labelsTr/<stem>.tif` (uint8 {0,1}) | instancesTr > 0. This is the jeong200-style binary surface label. |
| `ignoreTr/<stem>.tif` (uint8) | 1 = removed off-papyrus voxels (dilated 2) or CT material > clip(0.7·pitch, 4, 10) vox from every label (unlabelled sheets), restricted to unlabelled voxels. Optional for scorers. |
| `imagesTr/<stem>_0000.tif` | the 9.362 CT crop (uint8). |

Verified on 8 random in-set sites with `band_stats` over the 144³ core:
- binary values {0,1};
- thickness 3.0 vox (one site 2.5);
- band extent t = -1 to 1.
- **Difference from jeong200:** the CT half-max edge sits 0.3-4 vox outside the band ridge (median ≈ 2), so our band is about 1-2 vox deeper into the sheet than jeong200's. This follows from where the mesh is placed (the same offset was measured on the raw meshes). It matters for binary-surface Dice against jeong200-trained models. It does not matter for the instance and winding tests. I did not shift the labels, because the recto side is ambiguous in contact zones.

## 6. Scoring (eval/winding_sites.py --dataset p0139)
**Why the old rule is gameable** (coordinator warning).
- The jeong200 site test uses only adjacent pairs (expected step 1) found along the normal, with the site class defined by distance d, and the rule 0.5 ≤ delta/expected < 1.5.
- A constant field of pitch 16 vox with no image input gives delta = d/16 and scores 99.9% compressed / 100% wide, beating the trained model (73.9 / 80.8).
- The same holds here. On p0139's legacy normal-ray pairs, image-free pitch fields score 88-91% of wide pairs under the ratio rule (section 6 results).

**Pair design** (`eval/p0139_sites.scoring_pairs`, frozen in `data/eval/p0139_9um/scoring_pairs.csv`):
- A and B are any two cleaned surface voxels in the core with 2 ≤ |B - A| < 48 vox, both inside the one 128³ tile the scorer reads.
- Expected step = |w_B - w_A| in {0, 1, 2, 3, 4}. Step 0 means the same wrap, displaced along the sheet.
- Balancing: per site and per 4-vox distance bin, the groups {0, 1, ≥ 2} get equal counts (at most 100 per group per site). The Euclidean distance distribution is therefore the same for every group, so distance carries no information about the step.
  - Diagnostic: the best constant pitch on Euclidean distance (`const_euclid`) scores about 1/3, i.e. it calls every pair "1".
- Endpoints closer than 1 vox to another instance (coincident) are excluded.
- Each pair also carries:
  - `proj`: the projected distance along the local sheet normal, the mean of |v·n_A| and |v·n_B|, with normals from the structure tensor of each instance's own raster;
  - `contact`: true if either endpoint has another instance within 4 vox (Euclidean). Otherwise the pair is "open".

**Metrics:**
- **Primary:** pooled share of pairs with |delta - expected| < 0.25 wrap. Broken down by expected step (0-4 and the groups 0 / 1 / ≥ 2), by compression (contact / open) and by site class.
- Uncertainty: 95% CI from a site bootstrap.
- Unreadable predictions count as wrong.

**Baselines,** scored by the same code and reported next to the model:

| name | delta | role |
|---|---|---|
| `gt` | GT winding of the nearest surface voxel, cut into the same 128³ tiles and read by the same `site_delta` | plumbing check; must be 100% |
| `const_pitch` | proj / P, with one P fitted on the set to maximise the primary metric (optimistic) | |
| `oracle_pitch` | proj / (median GT adjacent spacing of that crop) | the bar |
| `const_euclid` | Euclidean distance / P | diagnostic |

- **Verdict rule:** a model resolves sheets only if it beats `oracle_pitch`, meaning the paired site-bootstrap 95% CI of the difference lies above 0. This is `model_resolves_sheets` in the JSON.
- **Secondary column:** the old ratio rule, labelled `secondary_ratio_rule_0.5_1.5_GAMEABLE`, with "correct" for step 0 defined as delta < 0.5.
- **Legacy block:** the old adjacent normal-ray pairs, labelled `legacy_adjacent_normal_rays_ratio_rule_GAMEABLE`, summarised by spacing class as before. Kept for continuity only.
- The jeong200 path is unchanged: same `find_sites` sampler with seed 0 and the same caps, so row order still matches `data/preds/sep_*.csv`.

**Commands:**
```
uv run python eval/p0139_sites.py                       # (re)freeze scoring_pairs.csv
uv run python eval/winding_sites.py --dataset p0139 --oracle --out data/preds/p0139_baselines   # GT + baselines

```

## 7. Leakage caveat
- These meshes are public PHerc0139 segments.
- The 2.4 um surface labels in `surface-labels-2026-07` (`surfaces/2um_032726/`) were rasterized from the same meshes.
- The undocumented Kaggle 320³ patches of that dataset have an ESRF ~9 um signature and may include PHerc0139.
- So any public baseline, such as the jeong / Kaggle surface models or nnU-Net checkpoints trained on surface-labels-2026-07, may have seen these sheets at 2.4 um or 9 um.
- Our own models trained only on synthetic data plus jeong200 are clean. A public model's score here is an upper bound on its generalisation.

## 9. Problems and limits
- **Loose meshes.** Mesh surfaces sit on average about 2 vox inside the recto half-max edge and wander 2-4 vox in places.
  - About 10% of raster voxels are removed as off papyrus (into ignore).
  - 97 of 200 candidates failed on papyrus, and only 3 pass a strict rule.
- **Unlabelled layers.**
  - Delaminations, segment holes, and wraps outside the w023-w059 range are papyrus with no label.
  - Unlabelled material is 0.7% / 8% / 66% (p10/p50/p90). `coverage_ok` is false on 21 of 87 sites.
  - Use `ignoreTr`. Do not score false positives on unlabelled material.
  - Winding-step scoring is unaffected: expected = |w_B - w_A| holds whatever lies between the sheets.
- **Compressed pairs are rarer in the raster than in the mining proxy** (about 1/3). Hence the "mixed" class, and the per-pair contact stratum in the metric.
- **The step ≥ 2 group** is mostly step 2. Steps 3-4 need short distances in compressed zones and are few. Within the ≥ 2 group, distance still separates 2 from 4.
- **The download figure** of 17.5 GB counts every 128³ chunk touched as a full 2.1 MB read, so it is an upper bound.
