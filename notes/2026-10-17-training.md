# 2026-10-17: ablation training (train/, cloud/)

## Datasets
- Dataset101_DS059real: the Dataset059 training subset (data/train/ds059/split.json), 662 cases. Fold 0 holds out
  a fixed 40 random cases (seed 0) for validation.
- Dataset102_SimV1: 400 randomized sim cells (20 scroll seeds 101-120 x 20 cells, `scripts/gen_sim.sh`,
  `sim/generate.py --randomize`), 7.91 um input with surface labels.
  - Preprocessed with Dataset101's plans (`nnUNetv2_move_plans_between_datasets`; `scripts/prep_ds102.sh`).
  - So normalization is identical: CTNormalization with real intensity statistics (mean 124.7, sd 40.35,
    p0.5 45, p99.5 229).
- nnU-Net v2 2.8.1, 3d_fullres, patch 128^3, batch 2, PlainConvUNet.

## Cloud run (Prime Intellect)
- Pod: 4x L40S, 128 vCPU, driver 580 (torch 2.14.1 cu130), $4.38/h, created ~21:55 UTC 2026-10-16.
  - `cloud/pod_setup.sh` builds the env, downloads the real patches (662/662) and preprocesses both datasets.
    Pod preprocessing matches local on a spot-checked case (same mean and std).
  - `cloud/pod_train.sh` runs one lane per GPU.
- CPU oversubscription: with default thread settings every augmentation worker spawned threads for all host cores
  (load ~400-550, no epoch in 12 min). Setting `OMP/MKL/OPENBLAS/NUMEXPR_NUM_THREADS=1` with 24 DA workers per lane
  gives 33 s/epoch (local RTX 3090: 109 s).
- SqueezeWarpTransform starved its GPU: building the warp field at full resolution on the 205^3 pre-crop took 2.4 s
  per sample on the busy pod.
  - The displacement is now built on a ~3 vox coarse grid and upsampled trilinearly.
  - Mean deviation from the full-resolution field is 0.002 vox (max 0.6 vox); parameter time drops from 0.32 to
    0.11 s locally.
  - Lane 1 was restarted alone via /root/lane1.sh, so pod_train.sh's TRAIN_DONE no longer covers squeeze250.
  - After the fix squeeze250 runs at 32.5 s/epoch, the same as the other lanes; all four GPUs are at 98-99%.
    The pod load average dropped from ~230 to ~56.

## Evaluation (`scripts/eval_arms.sh`)
- Runs locally on jeong200 (200 patches, ~4 s per patch on the RTX 3090, under a 6 GB lease):
  `eval/infer_nnunet.py --fold 0` -> `eval/separation.py` -> official `eval/score.py` (metric venv).
- Inference declares the model's plans spacing as the input spacing.
  - Our models are planned at nnU-Net's default spacing of 1.0, bshepp's at 7.91.
  - The old hardcoded 7.91 made nnU-Net try to resample our models' input to 2373^3.
- Copying `checkpoint_best.pth` while training rewrites it can produce a truncated zip: snapshot with `cp` on the
  pod first.

**Method gate (README): sim pretraining v1 fails.** simft vs squeeze250:
- compressed separation +0.9 pts (gate: +5);
- VOI_merge -1.7% (gate: -10%);
- official -0.008 (allowed: down by at most 0.01).

Other readings:
- SqueezeWarp augmentation does nothing measurable over real250: -0.9 pts, official +0.006.
- More real epochs help most: real350 is +2.2 pts over real250, within the CI but the best arm. The models are not yet
  converged at 250 epochs.
- All real-trained arms keep sheets apart ~92% of the time *when they predict the site*. The headroom is recall at
  compressed sites (~45% missed) and over-fragmentation (cc ~45 vs 13.5), not merging.
- bshepp's near-zero hit rate is abstention learned from Kaggle's ignore label (notes/2026-10-16-baseline-bshepp.md).
  A clean model trained on full labels reaches half the compressed sites.

**Why sim v1 didn't transfer (simpre zero-shot):**
- Its masks are too thick (fg 0.27 vs 0.17) and fuse at contacts (sep given hit 9.5%). The sim label convention does not
  match the real band: the real band is the first ~4.5 vox of the sheet from the recto face, with its own tolerance.
- Texture speckle: 441 components per patch, so real CT textures fire as sheets. A realism gap.
- Deeper reason: plain surface segmentation already has 662 real labelled patches. Sim adds little where real labels
  exist. Its unique value is labels real data *cannot* have: winding identity through welds, and arc-length
  coordinates. Next use of the sim: the winding-field model (notes/2026-10-18-campaign.md, W2), at 9 um.
