# PHerc0139 benchmark vs villa #2167 (2026-10-20)

villa #2167 (ChaseHendrick, opened 2026-10-20): in the public PHerc0139 meshes w035 covers ~260 deg of its turn; in the
missing ~95 deg sector (z 9000-27800 on 20260102150214) w036 lies inside w034 on 42 % of rays, and w033-w037 spacing is
~255 um vs ~560 um elsewhere. Label order there is unreliable (or the sector is genuinely compressed; CT not checked).

- 16 of 87 sites have an interior missing winding; none has w034 and w036 without w035.
- The 172 skip pairs are probably label errors (m7 also undercounts them); drop them in the next freeze.

- Inside the band the gain holds where GT masks are right (+3.48, n 8,102) and is larger where they are wrong (+8.33,

- Elsewhere ~0, including 5,095 compressed-class pairs.
- So the +2.1 pp headline is a w033-w037 effect, the band #2167 questions. It must be stated that way, and the band
  needs a CT-based check (#2167's gap sector vs the rest of the band) before it carries a claim.
