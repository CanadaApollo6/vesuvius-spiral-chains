# No-raise chains on PHerc0139 (2026-10-23)

Question: arm M2 (auto chains without the grad_mag raise step) passed the PHerc1667 confirmation
(notes/2026-10-22-confirmation-region.md §9-12). Does the same recipe help on PHerc0139's dev bands, with the current
pipeline? Band 1 is z 3000-4500 and band 2 is z 1750-3000. This is dev work, not pre-registered.

**Verdict: it depends on the zone and on what is counted.**
- **Global winding count: dropping raises hurts, on both bands.** Seam wrong area is worse than with no chains at all
  (band 1: +14.7 / +8.3 vs the two base seeds; band 2: +6.7 / +12.0). Against the gm arms it is +26.6 (band 1) and
  +15.7 (band 2), and against the oracle +28 / +26.
  - Without raises the chains undercount windings where the sheets are tightly packed. The global offset of the
    no-raise fit is 12 against 17 for the oracle and 18 for arm0.
  - This confirms what arm8 suggested.
- **Local, label-robust windings (own offsets): depends on the band.**
  - **Band 2: helps.** Against arm21 (gm 0.18), inner consistent wrong area is -9.8, outer -2.4, pairs +6.0 and
    switches -16. It ties the oracle on inner (-1.1 [-5.3, +2.8]) and is clearly better than both base seeds on inner
    (-5.6 / -5.3).
  - **Band 1: neutral overall, zone-dependent.** It ties arm23 and the oracle on inner consistent (+1.0 / +0.5).
    - Zone k43-50 improves: -21 vs arm23. It is pinned in the low state, like the oracle and arm29.
    - Zone k23-40 gets worse: +15.6 vs arm23, +9.7 vs the oracle. This loss sits in the label-inconsistent inner
      windings (+25.4); the consistent ones tie.
    - Pairs are slightly worse than gm (-3.2 [-6.3, -0.4]).
- **Bottom line for the write-up.** On PHerc0139 the M2 recipe does not reproduce the PHerc1667 win as a clean
  improvement:
  - it wins locally on band 2 and in band-1 zone k43-50;
  - it loses in band-1 zone k23-40;
  - it loses the global winding count everywhere.
  - Raises (or another count-correcting step) are still needed for the absolute winding number in the tightly packed
    PHerc0139 bands.

### Seed noise

Both no-raise seed pairs agree to about 1 pp or better on every metric. The largest shifts are pairs -1.1 and switches
-1.8 in band 2. This matches the chain-arm SDs in notes/2026-10-22-fit-seed-variance.md, so one fit per no-raise arm
would have sufficed. The base seeds still flip in the k43-50 / seam / outer metrics, so those deltas are given against
both base seeds.

## 4. Reading

- **Robust across both seeds and both base seeds:**
  - pairs vs base: band 1 +12-13, band 2 n.s.;
  - inner consistent vs base: band 1 -17 to -18, band 2 about -5.5;
  - seam vs base: worse everywhere;
  - band 2 vs arm21: better on pairs, inner, outer and switches.
- **Compared with PHerc1667.** There, M2's inner own-offset gain was -27 to -29 with pairs above the oracle, and M1
  (gm raises) did nothing.
  - On PHerc0139 band 1, gm raises already capture the inner gain: arm23 inner cons -19 vs base, the same as
    no-raise.
  - Removing the raises therefore buys k43-50 (low state) at the cost of k23-40 on the inconsistent windings, plus
    the global count.
  - On band 2, where the gm rule raises 25 % of steps (it is known to fail there, notes/2026-10-22-hitl-budget.md),
    removing raises is a clear local win.
- **What the global-count loss means for an entry.** The seam wrong area is +26 to +28 vs the oracle on both bands.
  An entry built on no-raise chains alone would carry absolute winding numbers off by several windings in these
  bands. A count-correcting step is still needed, and it must be better than the gm 0.18 rule on band 2. Options: CT
  gap audit, raises on a stricter rule, or a few human checks.
